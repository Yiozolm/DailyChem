# 化合物 C001：ethyl benzoate

化合物 C001（ethyl benzoate）为colorless oil。

将benzoic acid (1.0 mmol) 和 ethanol (5 mL)溶于ethanol，加入sulfuric acid (catalytic)。反应液在reflux下搅拌4 h。反应混合物经diluted with water and extracted with ethyl acetate。粗产物经column chromatography纯化，得到ethyl benzoate（120 mg, 63%）。

## 表征数据

1H NMR (400 MHz, CDCl3) δ 8.56 (s, 2H), 7.51 (s, 2H), 5.17 (s, 2H), 3.11 (s, 9H), 2.72 (s, 2H), 1.24 (s, 2H).

13C NMR (101 MHz, CDCl3) δ 165.2, 132.1, 129.5, 128.4, 52.1.

HRMS (ESI) m/z calculated for C9H10O2Na [M + Na]+ 173.0573, found 173.0570.
